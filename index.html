About Evil Larry Bake Shop
Our Wickedly Delicious Beginning

Long ago, a mischievous baker named Larry started experimenting with bold flavors that were “dangerously delicious.” Locals nicknamed him Evil Larry — not because he was truly evil, but because his creations were impossible to resist. Today, Evil Larry Bake Shop continues that tradition with daring recipes, playful twists, and baked goods that surprise and delight.

Our Mission

We believe baking should be more than food — it should be an experience. Our mission is to craft treats that spark joy, bring people together, and make every celebration unforgettable.

What Makes Us Special
  • • Freshly Baked Daily - Every cake, pastry, and loaf is made from scratch each morning.
  • • Premium Ingredients - Real butter, farm-fresh eggs, and natural flavors only.
  • • Creative Craftsmanship - Hand-decorated cakes and pastries with artisan detail.
  • • Unique Flavors - Signature recipes that blend tradition with innovation.
Meet the Team

Behind every loaf and cupcake is a passionate crew of bakers, decorators, and servers who love what they do. Together, we bring warmth, creativity, and dedication to every batch.

Community Connection

We're proud to be part of the local community — sourcing ingredients from nearby farms, supporting events, and sharing our love of baking with everyone who walks through our doors.

Our Promise

At Evil Larry Bake Shop, the only “evil” is how hard it is to stop at just one bite. We promise freshness, quality, and a touch of mischief in every treat.
